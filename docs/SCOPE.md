# Mathematical scope

The joint theorem is `Tusnady.Integrated.main_sharp_order`. For each fixed
dimension d ≥ 2, the worst-case discrepancy of N points for closed lower
orthants and for bounded closed axis-parallel boxes has sharp order
(log N)^(d − 1/2). Points range over all of real d-dimensional space.

The development includes the higher-dimensional lower bound, Nikolov’s upper
bound, Wei’s planar lower bound, and the comparison between the two box
families. The polytope and algorithmic results of §8 are outside the scope
of this release. See the [statement-to-source mapping](INTEGRATED_MANUSCRIPT_MAP.md).
